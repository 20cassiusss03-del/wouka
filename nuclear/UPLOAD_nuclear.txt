Nuclear Plants Hired Him for 12 Minutes

ОПИСАНИЕ
Twelve minutes inside a steam generator can use up a nuclear worker's entire legal radiation dose for the year. For decades, American plants hired temporary "jumpers" to do exactly that, and the industry had nicknames for them: glow boys, gamma sponges, nuclear nomads.

This is the story of who they were, why plants paid them for minutes instead of years, what the same system looked like in Japan after Fukushima, and why reactors coming back online today still need people willing to take the dose.
